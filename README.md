# ai-automation
AI skills, automation workflows, and projects in artificial intelligence.

## [Job Scorer](https://github.com/capncapes/ai-automation/tree/main/projects/job-scorer)
Analyze and score open jobs against the user's work experience and preferences.

Create a project with your favorite AI assistant, drop the contents of `instructions.txt` into the project instructions, and upload context documents like a resume and candidate profile.

## [resume-update](https://github.com/capncapes/ai-automation/tree/main/skills/resume-update)
Drafts resumes, cover letters, LinkedIn content, and prospecting emails. Triggers on: 'update my resume', 'draft a cover letter', 'draft an email to the hiring manager', and requests to generate content based on the user's professional background.
